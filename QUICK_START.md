# Birthday page quick start

1. Keep index.html, styles.css, and script.js together.
2. Keep the six birthday photos and three video files in the images folder.
3. Open index.html in a browser.

The opening birthday card appears as soon as the page loads. Select **Open your surprise** to view the page and its photo memories, birthday note, wishes, and video messages.

## Media files used

- Photos: images/1.jpeg through images/6.jpeg
- Main video: images/main_video.mp4
- Friends' videos: images/Your_Friends.mp4 and images/my_friend.mp4

The page is responsive and includes fallback fonts if Google Fonts cannot be reached.
